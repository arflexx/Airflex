# Seller KYC Onboarding & Status Lifecycle

Seller verification is required before publishing P2P trade listings on AirFlex. This process protects both buyers and sellers by verifying the real-world identity of merchants engaging in high-volume airtime, data, and fiat settlements.

---

## 1. Prerequisites Before Submission

Before initiating the verification process, ensure you have:
1. **Authenticated Account**: You must be signed in with an active phone number verified via OTP.
2. **Supported Identity Document**:
   - Nigeria: National Identification Number (NIN) slip/card, Driver's License, International Passport, or Permanent Voter's Card (PVC).
   - Identity document details must exactly match the legal name and date of birth provided in the application.
3. **High-Resolution Photo/Selfie**:
   - Captured in good lighting with full face visibility.
   - Format: JPEG or PNG only (PDF documents are not accepted).
   - Maximum file size: **5 MB**.

## 3. KYC Status Lifecycle & State Transitions

The diagram below outlines the full lifecycle of a user's verification profile:

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Unverified / New User] -->|Submit KYC Form| B[Pending Review]
    B -->|Admin Approves (24-48h SLA)| C[Verified / Approved]
    B -->|Admin Rejects| D[Rejected]
    D -->|Correct Details & Resubmit| B
    C -->|Annual Expiry (365 Days)| E[Expired]
    E -->|Submit Renewal KYC| B
```

### Detailed Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning & User Impact | Platform Access Level |
|---|---|---|
| **`Unverified`** | Initial account state. No verification submitted. | Can browse marketplace and purchase airtime/data as buyer. Cannot create seller listings. |
| **`Pending`** | Submission received and queued for compliance review. | Application locked against duplicate edits until review concludes. Existing buyer permissions remain active. |
| **`Verified`** | KYC approved by the compliance team. | Full seller privileges unlocked: create escrow listings, execute high-volume sales, and access merchant API tools. |
| **`Rejected`** | Submission failed verification criteria (e.g., blurry photo, name mismatch, invalid NIN). | Seller permissions withheld. User can review errors and resubmit following cooldown rules. |
| **`Expired`** | Verification exceeded validity window (12 months from approval). | Seller privileges temporarily suspended (active listings paused). Restored immediately upon renewal approval. |

---

## 4. Review SLA & Turnaround Timelines

* **Standard SLA**: Initial submissions are reviewed within **24 to 48 business hours**.
* **Weekend & Holiday Ingestion**: Submissions made over weekends or national public holidays are processed on the subsequent business day.
* **Notification**: Users receive real-time notifications via in-app banner and SMS alert upon status transition (`Verified` or `Rejected`).

---

## 5. Expiry Behavior & Annual Renewal

* **Expiry Period**: Verification remains valid for **365 calendar days** from the date of approval.
* **Advance Notice**: AirFlex dispatches email and in-app reminder notices at **30 days** and **7 days** prior to credential expiration.
* **Grace Period & Locked Features**:
  * Upon reaching `Expired` state, active marketplace listings are automatically hidden from the public order book.
  * Escrow funds currently in active or disputed trades remain safe and proceed to normal completion.
  * The seller must submit an updated selfie and confirm current identity records to transition back to `Verified`.

---

## 6. Re-Submission Rules & Rate Limiting

To safeguard verification infrastructure against automated spam and Denial-of-Service attacks, submissions are protected by database-backed sliding-window rate limiting implemented in `server/src/middleware/rateLimitKyc.ts`.

### Rate Limit Policy
* **Maximum Submissions**: **3 submissions** (`KYC_MAX_SUBMISSIONS`) per user.
* **Sliding Window**: **60 minutes** (`KYC_WINDOW_MINUTES`).
* **Rate Limit Exceeded (HTTP 429)**: If a user exceeds 3 submission attempts in an hour, the API returns:
  ```json
  {
    "status": "error",
    "message": "Too many KYC submission attempts. Please wait before trying again.",
    "retryAfterSeconds": 1800
  }
  ```
  The response includes standard `Retry-After` headers indicating the remaining seconds before a new attempt is permitted.

### Re-Submission Steps After Rejection
1. Review the in-app feedback regarding the rejection.
2. Confirm your legal name spelling matches your document character-for-character.
3. Re-capture your identity photo ensuring:
   - Zero glare or lens reflections.
   - All four corners of the physical card are visible.
   - Text is sharp and machine-readable.
4. Resubmit via the **Complete KYC** form in the user dashboard. Once submitted, status returns to **`Pending`** for priority re-review.
